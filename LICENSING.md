## Versions already distributed

A license decision for a future version cannot revoke rights already granted
for a version received under AGPL-3.0. Recipients may continue to exercise those
rights subject to the AGPL terms. No roadmap, edition name, policy file, or
commercial discussion changes the license of a copy already distributed.

## Possible future versions

The project is evaluating a Dify-style **Open Core / source-available** model.
That is a non-operative plan, not the current license. Any different terms
would apply prospectively only when all of the following are complete:

1. qualified counsel has approved the final license and public description;
2. the copyright and contribution chain confirms authority to relicense every
   affected part;
3. third-party notices and incompatible material have been resolved;
4. the Community/Hosted/Enterprise boundary has been reviewed; and
5. a future release clearly identifies its effective license and version.

The planning requirements in
[legal/licensing/FUTURE_SOURCE_AVAILABLE_REQUIREMENTS.md](legal/licensing/FUTURE_SOURCE_AVAILABLE_REQUIREMENTS.md)
are not license text and have no current legal effect.

## Contributions and provenance

Repository presence and Git authorship do not prove copyright ownership.
Contributors must disclose copied, generated, or third-party material and its
source, revision, and license. A Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off is a
provenance attestation; it does not override AGPL-3.0 and does not silently grant
future relicensing rights. If contribution terms change, contributors will
receive clear notice before those terms apply.

## Commercial questions

AGPL-3.0 already permits commercial use subject to its terms. Questions about
separate support or commercial arrangements may be sent to
[support@agentsdance.ai](mailto:support@agentsdance.ai) with the subject
`Commercial licensing`. Only a signed agreement from an authorized party can
create separate rights; support correspondence alone does not alter a published
license.

Trademark permission is separate from copyright permission. See
[TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).
