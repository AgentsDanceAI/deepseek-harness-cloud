# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, consent, and attribution.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Put community safety ahead of winning an argument.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern;
- abuse of project access, moderation authority, or confidential reports; or
- conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, events,
and private project communications, and when someone officially represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[support@agentsdance.ai](mailto:support@agentsdance.ai) with the subject
`Code of Conduct`. Include links or screenshots only when doing so is safe and
lawful. For a security vulnerability, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will protect confidentiality as far as practical, avoid retaliation,
and use proportionate responses that may include a private warning, content
removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. A maintainer with a
conflict of interest must recuse themselves. If a report concerns the normal
enforcement contact, escalate it to
[legal@agentsdance.ai](mailto:legal@agentsdance.ai).

Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this code, but an
unsubstantiated good-faith report will not be treated as misconduct.
