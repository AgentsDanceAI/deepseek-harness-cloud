# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective date: August 17, 2026**

This Policy is an integral part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to every form of deepseek-harness-cloud: the cloud workspace, the desktop client, and the model gateway.

This service can execute code, access networks, and read and write files. The greater the capability, the more clearly the boundaries have to be written down.

---

## 1. Absolutely Prohibited

The following conduct results in account termination as soon as it is discovered, with no refund, and referral to law enforcement where necessary:

- **Child sexual exploitation**: generating, distributing, or soliciting any sexualized content involving minors;
- **Violence and terrorism**: planning, inciting, or assisting the commission of violence or terrorist attacks, or the manufacture of weapons (including biological, chemical, radiological, nuclear, and conventional weapons);
- **Malware and cyberattacks**: writing or distributing ransomware, trojans, worms, or botnets; conducting scanning, penetration testing, denial-of-service, or credential brute-forcing against any system without written authorization;
- **Unauthorized access**: bypassing the authentication or access controls of another party's systems;
- **Circumventing this service's mechanisms**: bypassing or tampering with metering, quotas, the login wall, container isolation, or security controls; attempting to extract our model providers' keys.

## 2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

- **Production of disinformation at scale**: bulk generation of fake news or forged official statements intended to mislead the public;
- **Impersonation**: impersonating another person, a business, or a government agency, including generating forged identity documents, certificates, or financial instruments convincing enough to pass as genuine;
- **Harassment and hate**: harassment, threats, or doxxing directed at individuals; hate speech based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar grounds;
- **Spam**: bulk generation or delivery of unsolicited commercial messages, SEO spam, or fake reviews;
- **Privacy violations**: collecting, inferring, or publishing another person's personal information without authorization; performing biometric identification or surveillance on individuals;
- **Intellectual property infringement**: reproducing copyrighted works for the purpose of circumventing licensing;
- **Academic and exam cheating**: ghostwriting or sitting exams on someone else's behalf where this is expressly prohibited.

## 3. Restrictions on High-Risk Uses

In the following scenarios, the output of this service **must not be relied on as the sole basis** for a decision; a qualified human must make the final judgment:

- medical diagnosis, treatment plans, medication advice;
- legal opinions and litigation strategy;
- financial investment advice, credit approval;
- eligibility decisions in employment, housing, education, and insurance;
- control of critical infrastructure (power, transportation, water supply, medical devices).

Generative models produce output that looks plausible but is wrong. **The responsibility for acting on the output is yours.**

## 4. Additional Rules for the Cloud Workspace

The cloud workspace gives each user an isolated container that can execute arbitrary code. You **must not**:

- use it for cryptocurrency mining, reselling distributed computing, or any other consumption of compute unrelated to agent tasks;
- use it as a proxy server, a VPN exit, or a traffic relay;
- host long-running services exposed to the outside (it is not a web host; idle containers are suspended automatically);
- store or distribute unlawful content;
- attempt to break out of the container boundary to reach the host machine or other users' containers.

Links generated by the port preview feature are **accessible only to you**. Do not treat them as a channel for public publication.

## 5. Resale and Redistribution

- You **must not** resell, sublicense, or in any form redistribute the model access provided by this service;
- You **must not** offer this service to third parties as the backend of your own product, unless you have obtained our separate written permission;
- You may deploy our open source code yourself and connect **your own** model keys — that is not restricted by this section.

## 6. Reporting and Enforcement

To report a violation, email **security@agentsdance.ai** (security issues) or **support@agentsdance.ai** (everything else).

How we handle it depends on severity:

| Severity | Response |
|---|---|
| Minor or apparently accidental | A notice and a chance to correct it |
| Repeated or clear violation | Restriction of features or account suspension |
| Conduct listed in Section 1 | Immediate termination, no refund, and reporting to law enforcement where necessary |

Except in urgent situations (an attack in progress, unlawful conduct, imminent harm), we will notify you first and explain the reason. You may appeal by replying to the email, and a different colleague will review it.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

Material changes to this Policy will be announced at least 15 days before they take effect, through an in-product notice and to your registered email address.
