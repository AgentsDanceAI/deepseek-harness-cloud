# Refund Policy

**Version 1.0 · Effective date: August 17, 2026**

This policy is an integral part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and is displayed together with them at checkout.

---

## 0. First, to be clear: this service does not auto-renew

**We do not initiate any recurring charge against your payment method.** Every payment is a single, one-time charge: when the period ends, the account automatically falls back to the free plan, and unless you place another order yourself, there will be no second charge. There is therefore no "charged because you forgot to cancel" situation, and no subscription to cancel.

## 1. First purchase: 14-day refund, no reason required

Within **14 calendar days** of the date of your **first** payment for this service, if **no more than 20%** of the credits included in that purchase have been consumed, you may request a **full refund**.

- "First purchase" means the first successful payment under your account; it applies to both plans and credit packs;
- The consumption percentage is determined by the usage records in our backend, which you can check yourself in the console;
- After a refund, the credits and machine hours granted by that purchase are withdrawn at the same time.

## 2. Situations with an unconditional full refund

In the following situations we refund in full, whether or not the service has been used and whether or not it is a first purchase:

| Situation | Description |
|---|---|
| Duplicate charge | The same order was charged more than once |
| Incorrect charge | A charge you did not initiate, or an amount that does not match what was displayed |
| Non-delivery | Payment succeeded but the entitlements were not issued, and we cannot fix it within 24 hours |

## 3. Prorated refund for service interruption

If this service becomes unavailable for **more than 72 consecutive hours in a single incident**, we refund the fee for the current period in proportion to the number of affected days within that period. You do not need to provide evidence; we check the availability records on our own initiative.

## 4. When an account is terminated

- **Terminated for reasons on our side** (for example, if we cease operations): we refund the full periods you have paid for that have not yet started, and handle the period in progress under Section 3.
- **Terminated because you breached the Terms**: we refund the full periods you have paid for that have **not yet started**; the period in progress and credits already issued but unused are not refunded.
- **Terminated for fraud, attacks, or unlawful conduct**: no amount is refunded.

## 5. Situations with no refund

Apart from the sections above, the following are not refundable:

- Credits already consumed and machine hours already used (model calls and compute have already incurred actual costs);
- Allowances granted free of charge (they were never paid for);
- The balance of unused allowance at the end of a period — allowances **do not carry over** to the next period, and cannot be cashed out or transferred;
- The remaining paid period when you close your account yourself (please use it up or request a refund before closing).

## 6. How to apply

Send an email to **support@agentsdance.ai** stating:

1. Your registered email address;
2. The order number (available on the "Orders" page in the console);
3. The reason for your request.

**We respond within 5 business days.** Approved refunds are returned by **Waffo Pancake** along the original payment route to your original payment method; the time to arrive depends on your card issuer and is typically 5–10 business days.

## 7. Disputes

If you disagree with the outcome, you may reply to the original email to request a review, and a different colleague will reassess it. Your rights under the consumer protection laws of your jurisdiction are not limited by this policy. You may also contact your card issuer directly to initiate a dispute, but we recommend talking to us first — most issues can be resolved faster that way.
