# Counsel review checklist

Every unchecked item keeps
`LICENSE_STATE.toml:transition_status` blocked. Completion requires a non-secret
evidence or review ID, reviewer, and date; a checkmark without evidence is not
approval.

- [ ] Licensor identity and complete chain of title — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Per-file copyright inventory reconciled to the release tree — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Every third-party component, asset, font, icon, generated file, and embedded notice reviewed — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Existing contributor grants and imported history reviewed — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] DCO versus CLA decision and contributor notice approved — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] AGPL source, notice, network-use, compatibility, and transition obligations analyzed — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Managed-service, multi-tenant, white-label, and attribution language drafted narrowly — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Community baseline and non-paywalled security/export/backup obligations verified — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Consumer, employment, export-control, sanctions, privacy, and competition implications reviewed — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Trademark policy and product naming reviewed without unsupported registration claims — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Effective date, prospective version boundary, and upgrade/downgrade behavior approved — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Public edition comparison and “source available” description approved — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Final operative text and notices delivered as a signed approval artifact — evidence/review ID:
- [ ] Release owner separately authorizes the exact future version and artifacts — evidence/review ID:

## Required conclusion

Counsel's conclusion must identify which material can be relicensed, which must
remain under its existing license, any required source/notices, the exact future
effective version, and any claims the project must not make. It must also confirm
that no action purports to revoke AGPL rights already granted.
